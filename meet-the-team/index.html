Our Story

Better living starts with us.

At Aacropolis Homes, we have spent over thirty years working closely with our clients to bring their unique visions to life. Our commitment to quality craftsmanship and thoughtful design ensures that each home reflects both style and comfort. Whether we're building in vibrant new communities or infill homes in established neighbourhoods, we take pride in delivering value and creating lasting relationships.

MEET THE TEAM

Casey Sharkey

Area Sales Manager

With a background in stakeholder coordination and project management, Casey brings clarity, foresight and close attention to detail to the homebuilding process. She joined Aacropolis Homes in June 2025, drawn to the opportunity to work one-on-one with families and translate the way they live, what they value and what they envision for the future into a home designed around them. Casey is passionate about creating clarity and confidence throughout the building journey. She believes the best outcomes begin with meaningful conversations, transparent communication and careful documentation. By keeping each family’s priorities visible throughout the process, she helps ensure the finished home remains connected to the reasons they chose to build it.

Fun fact: Casey has found more than 100 four-leaf clovers throughout her lifetime. She has always had an eye for noticing the special details others might walk past.
